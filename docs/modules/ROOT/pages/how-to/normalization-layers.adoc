= Choose a normalization layer
:description: The three normalization layers of the SKaiNET network DSL — batchNorm, groupNorm and layerNorm — what each one averages over, and when to prefer GroupNorm.

The network DSL has three normalization layers. All three compute
`(x - mean) / sqrt(variance + eps)` and then, unless switched off, apply a learned scale
(gamma) and shift (beta). They differ in *which elements share one mean and variance*.

The snippet on this page is compiled and executed in CI from `skainet-docs-samples`
(`NormalizationLayers.kt`).

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Builder | Statistics are taken over | Typical use

| `batchNorm(numFeatures)`
| the batch, per feature / channel
| convolutional nets trained with large batches

| `groupNorm(numGroups, numChannels)`
| one sample, per group of channels (and all spatial positions)
| small or varying batch sizes: detection, segmentation, diffusion U-Nets

| `layerNorm(normalizedShape)`
| one sample, over the trailing `normalizedShape` dimensions
| transformers and other sequence models
|===

== groupNorm

`groupNorm(numGroups, numChannels)` splits the `numChannels` channels of an `[N, C, *]` input
into `numGroups` groups of `C / numGroups` consecutive channels and normalizes each group of
each sample on its own. `numChannels` must be divisible by `numGroups`.

Optional arguments: `eps` (default `1e-5`) and `affine` (default `true`; `false` drops gamma
and beta).

=== When to prefer it over batchNorm

Batch normalization estimates the mean and variance from the batch, so its error grows as
the batch shrinks; with one or two samples per batch the estimate is too noisy to train
with. Group normalization does not depend on the batch size. Wu and He report that its
accuracy stays stable over a wide range of batch sizes, and that at a batch size of 2 it has
a 10.6 % lower ImageNet error than batch normalization with ResNet-50
(https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.08494[Wu & He, _Group Normalization_, 2018]). Use `groupNorm`
when memory limits the batch size (high-resolution images, video, detection and
segmentation heads) or when batches at inference time do not look like training batches.

Two settings of `numGroups` are known layers in their own right:

* `numGroups = 1` normalizes over all channels and positions of a sample
  (layer normalization over `[C, H, W]`);
* `numGroups = numChannels` normalizes each channel of each sample separately
  (instance normalization).

=== Semantics

The layer follows `torch.nn.GroupNorm`: the variance is the biased one (divided by the number
of elements in the group), `eps` is added to the variance before the square root, and gamma
and beta have one value per channel. `GroupNormalizationTest` compares the output with
PyTorch reference values for 1, 2, 3 and 6 groups; the script that produced them is
`scripts/reference/groupnorm_reference.py`.
